Welcome to S.I.T.R.A

S.I.T.R.A - Shaping Imagination To Reality Anywhere. At S.I.T.R.A, we combine the excitement of flea market entrepreneurship with cutting-edge 3D printing technology. We're passionate about creating unique, high-quality 3D printed products and providing comprehensive 3D printing services to bring your ideas to life.

What We Do

Flea Market Sales: We specialize in selling beautifully crafted 3D printed toys and ornaments at flea markets. Our collection features everything from extendable telescoping weapons and flexible action figures to festive holiday decorations and Halloween props. Each piece is carefully designed and printed with attention to detail and quality.

3D Printing Services: Beyond our product line, we offer a wide range of custom 3D printing services tailored to your needs:

  • Auto Repairing Solutions: Custom 3D printed replacement parts for vehicles with precise fit and durability
  • Customized Busts: Detailed 3D portrait busts from photos, perfect for memorials, awards, or personal keepsakes
  • Customized Pet Equipments: Personalized pet accessories and equipment including tags, holders, and custom pet accessories with custom designs
  • Workshop Tools: Custom jigs, holders, and workshop accessories designed to your specifications
  • Home Solutions: Functional household items like organizers, hooks, and decorative accessories
  • And Everything Else You Can Imagine: From custom art pieces to specialized tools, if you can envision it, we can help bring it to life with 3D printing technology

Our mission is to make 3D printing accessible and affordable while maintaining the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship. Whether you're looking for a unique toy at the flea market or need a custom solution for your specific requirements, S.I.T.R.A is here to help turn your imagination into reality.

Portrait Busts

Create stunning 3D portrait busts from your photos with our advanced 3D printing technology. Our customized portrait process transforms your favorite photographs into detailed, lifelike sculptures that capture every nuance of expression and character.

1. Photo Submission

You provide us with high-quality photos from multiple angles - front, side, and optional profile views. The more reference images, the better we can capture your unique features.

2. 3D Modeling

Our skilled artists use advanced photogrammetry and 3D modeling software to create a detailed digital 3D model that accurately represents facial structure, expressions, and distinguishing features.

3. Design Refinement

We work with you to refine the model, ensuring it captures the essence and personality of the subject. Adjustments can be made to proportions, expressions, and fine details.

4. 3D Printing

Once approved, your portrait is printed using high-resolution 3D printing technology, layer by layer, with attention to surface quality and dimensional accuracy.

5. Finishing & Post-Processing

The bust undergoes careful post-processing - sanding, smoothing, and optional painting or coloring to achieve the desired finish, whether matte, glossy, or natural material appearance.

The result is a memorial-quality portrait bust that serves as a meaningful keepsake, tribute, or artistic statement piece. Perfect for honoring loved ones, celebrating achievements, or creating personalized art for your home or office.
